# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone who participates in this project.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Being respectful and considerate.
* Communicating constructively.
* Accepting constructive feedback.
* Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and other inappropriate conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action in response to behavior that violates this Code of Conduct.
